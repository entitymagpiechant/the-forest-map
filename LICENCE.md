### 🗺️ Map & Navigation
- Full World Map — reveal entire island with terrain and points of interest
- Player Position — real-time location marker
- Cave Locator — mark all cave entrances and exits
- Waypoint System — set custom markers for navigation

### 📦 Resource & Loot ESP
- Resource ESP — highlight trees, rocks, bushes, and animals
- Loot Tracker — show items, weapons, and supplies
- Container ESP — highlight suitcases, crates, and backpacks
- Building ESP — show player-built structures

### 👁️ Enemy & Survival
- Enemy Radar — detect cannibals and mutants through walls
- Health & Stamina Bars — overlay vital stats
- Co-op Support — works in multiplayer sessions
- No Fog — remove fog for better visibility
